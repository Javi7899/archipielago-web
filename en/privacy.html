What data Broken Archipelago collects

None. There are no accounts of our own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game sends nothing to any server of the developer.

Your progress

Levels, stars, heroes, skins, coins and gems stay only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the game.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your profile, your friends and the trophy leaderboard. In online games the devices send each other only the moves of the game, through Game Center. The developer does not receive your identity.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. The developer does not receive your payment details or your identity.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to the address on the support page.
