Broken Archipelago: Parkour 3D for iPhone and iPad

Broken Archipelago is a 3D action parkour game for iPhone and iPad. Every level is a crossing over a broken island: jump between platforms that move or crumble, dodge traps and fight through its creatures to the door at the end. Every fifth level a guardian keeps it shut.

There are 75 parkour levels across five worlds (Skull Coast, Lost Jungle, Coral Abyss, Frost Kingdom and Nebula Station) and 75 more battle levels, with a boss every fifth one. The 15 heroes truly play differently: each has three attacks with their own mechanics and a power of their own for the crossings. There are also 1v1 and 2v2 duels against bots or online with friends through Game Center, and a season pass with new prizes every 28 days.

The game is free, with no ads. Every hero can be earned by playing; gems, a starter pack and the premium pass can be bought in the game to go faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do I aim?

Drag the attack button where you want to fire and let go. The basic attack keeps firing while you hold it. If you drag back to the centre before letting go, the skill is not spent.

How do I get more heroes?

A new one joins every five crossing levels. If you don't want to wait, you can bring one early for 180 gems, which you earn from daily missions and the trophy road.

How do I play with a friend?

Both sign in to Game Center, go to Battles, pick online co-op, 1v1 or 2v2 and invite them. You both need the same version of the game.

I changed devices and lost my progress

Progress is saved only on the device. Bought gems can't be restored because they are spent; if a purchase never arrived, write to the address below.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the crossings and battles against bots. You only need a connection to play online and to buy.

What is the season pass?

Every 28 days a season of 30 tiers starts, and you climb it by playing: crossings, stars, battles, missions and the daily reward. Every tier has a free prize; the premium track, bought once per season, adds four skins, gems and coins, and gives you at once everything you have already reached.

The last levels are really hard

That's on purpose: the difficulty climbs slowly up to levels 70 to 75. Level up your hero with coins, take spells into the fight and try other heroes: each attacks differently and some suit each boss better.
