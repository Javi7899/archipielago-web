Broken Archipelago: 3D hero game

Frequently asked questions Contact Privacy policy

Broken Archipelago is a 3D action adventure for iPhone and iPad. Every level is a crossing over a broken island: jump between platforms that move or crumble, dodge traps and fight through its creatures to the door at the end. Every fifth level a guardian keeps it shut.

There are 75 levels across five worlds (Skull Coast, Lost Jungle, Coral Abyss, Frost Kingdom and Nebula Station) and 15 heroes that each play differently, each with a basic attack, a skill and an ultimate. There are also wave battles, co-op and duels against bots or online with friends through Game Center.

The game is free, with no ads. Everything can be earned by playing; gems can be bought in the game to go faster.

Frequently asked questions

How do I aim?

Drag the attack button where you want to fire and let go. The basic attack keeps firing while you hold it. If you drag back to the centre before letting go, the skill is not spent.

How do I get more heroes?

A new one joins every five crossing levels. If you don't want to wait, you can bring one early for 180 gems, which you earn from daily missions and the trophy road.

How do I play with a friend?

Both sign in to Game Center, go to Battles, pick online co-op, 1v1 or 2v2 and invite them. You both need the same version of the game.

I changed devices and lost my progress

Progress is saved only on the device. Bought gems can't be restored because they are spent; if a purchase never arrived, write to the address below.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the crossings and battles against bots. You only need a connection to play online and to buy.
